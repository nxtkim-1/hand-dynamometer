GripCheck

    How strong is your grip?

    This test measures how hard you can squeeze, how quickly your force rises, and how well you keep squeezing over time.

    1. 1
      Six short squeezes
      Three per hand, alternating, five seconds each.
    2. 2
      One long squeeze
      Thirty seconds with your dominant hand.
    3. 3
      Your report
      Compared with people of your age and sex, and saved as a PDF.

    Educational project. This is not a medical device and does not give a diagnosis.

    Online demo. No device is connected here, so each squeeze is simulated from a realistic force curve. Please do not enter real names or personal information.
